Context, without conclusions

When a symptom is recorded within six hours after the same product or activity on at least two entries, CareCard highlights that timing.

This can help you prepare questions for your appointment. It does not show that a product caused a symptom, and it should not guide medication changes.

Privacy & your data

This single-user app stores notes and photos in data/entries.json on the computer running the server. Files are not encrypted. Anyone with access to that computer may be able to read them.

When configured, Gemini automatically receives note text and recent symptom context on save, and selected notes to prepare the summary. The label button sends the selected photo. AI can make mistakes. Voice transcription uses your browser’s speech service, which may send audio to its provider. Use text if you prefer.

Vibration depends on your browser and device. Reduced-motion preferences also disable animated feedback.

Export a copy before deleting. Removing an entry also removes its attached image from the journal file; exported copies and backups must be deleted separately. CareCard does not claim clinical validation or HIPAA compliance.
